## Research character

What is genuinely supported: an executable conditioned-learning framework,
audited negative task qualification, predeclared recovery screens, three-seed
bounded Minecart task confirmation and two prospective Lunar confirmations.
Negative findings, raw vectors, budget categories, final-only checkpoints and
pilot/confirmation separation make the investigation reproducible and falsifiable.

What is not supported: a novel PPO/GAE/PopArt method, adaptive advantage over
compute-matched controls, a unique causal explanation of the failed backbone,
arbitrary preference controllability or environment/dynamics generalization.
Passing software tests and reproducing task landings do not fill these gaps.
RQ1–4 coverage remains explicit; the original allocation-study matrix is incomplete.
