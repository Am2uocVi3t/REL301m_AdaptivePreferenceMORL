## Explain the research accurately

The project began with preference-conditioned vector Q-learning and adaptive
allocation signals. The primary block completed 30 jobs and retained 28,800
validation episodes across both environments. Minecart's two ore components
were zero across its 14,400 primary validation episodes, so the comparative
qualification failed. This does not show that all DQN methods fail on Minecart.

Later, separately declared PPO studies demonstrated specific task gates:
three Minecart training seeds passed both profiles in both inference modes;
two prospective Lunar seeds passed the trained-profile landing gate. The Lunar
actor displayed in the demo is the earlier single-seed specialist pilot.
These results do not establish adaptive-allocation benefit or universal
preference control. GAE is Lunar-specific in this implementation.
