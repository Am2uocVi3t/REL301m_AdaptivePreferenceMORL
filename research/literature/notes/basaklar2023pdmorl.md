# PD-MORL: Preference-Driven Multi-Objective Reinforcement Learning Algorithm

Primary source: https://openreview.net/forum?id=zS9sRyaPFlJ

## Access report

OpenReview full text checked on 2026-10-15.

## Relevance and mechanism

PD-MORL uses a universal conditioned network, preference alignment/prediction components, and parallel training for continuous-action Pareto policies.

## Evidence and relationship to claims

The experiments assess Pareto quality and adaptation, not a factorial comparison of preference allocation signals. It establishes prior art for conditioned policy learning and bounds `combined-allocation`.

## Limitations and falsifiers

Architecture and action spaces complicate direct comparison. Equivalent preference scheduling in this method would narrow the project's distinction.
