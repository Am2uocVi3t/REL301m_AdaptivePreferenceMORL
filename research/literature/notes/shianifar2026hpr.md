# Hindsight Preference Replay Improves Preference-Conditioned Multi-Objective Reinforcement Learning

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.11604

## Access report

Author-posted full text, identified by its authors as accepted at AICS 2025, checked on 2026-10-15.

## Relevance and mechanism

HPR relabels replay samples with hindsight preferences derived from achieved vector returns, reusing experience across preferences.

## Evidence and relationship to claims

Preference-conditioned experiments compare replay strategies. This constrains broad sample-efficiency novelty for `combined-allocation`, but replay relabeling differs from choosing which preference gets new rollout/update budget.

## Limitations and falsifiers

The accessible artifact is author posted, so venue status must be stated accurately. Publication-scale work may need a combined replay/allocation comparison.
