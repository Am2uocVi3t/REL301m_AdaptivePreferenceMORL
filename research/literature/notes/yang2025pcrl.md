# Preference Controllable Reinforcement Learning with Advanced Multi-Objective Optimization

Primary source: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/yang25ax.html

## Access report

Open PMLR full text checked on 2026-10-15.

## Relevance and mechanism

PCRL/PreCo trains a preference-controllable meta-policy with a multi-objective gradient method that handles conflicts among objectives and preferences.

## Evidence and relationship to claims

ICML experiments assess controllability and Pareto performance, not an allocation-signal factorial. It further rules out broad preference-conditioning novelty for `combined-allocation`.

## Limitations and falsifiers

Algorithm and action-space differences complicate direct matching. Equivalent implicit preference scheduling would require revising the map.

Evidence pointer: method, optimization analysis, and experiments.
