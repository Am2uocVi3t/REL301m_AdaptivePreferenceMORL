# Automatic Curriculum Learning through Value Disagreement

Primary source: https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2020/hash/566f0ea4f6c2e947f36795c8f58ba901-Abstract.html

## Access report

Official NeurIPS full text checked on 2026-10-15.

## Relevance and mechanism

Independent value estimators form an ensemble; predictive dispersion estimates epistemic uncertainty and selects goals near the competence frontier.

## Evidence and relationship to claims

Goal-curriculum experiments establish disagreement allocation as known. `disagreement-allocation` transfers it to preferences with shared masked heads, so calibration and future-improvement prediction—not novelty of disagreement—must be tested.

## Limitations and falsifiers

Shared features can correlate heads and understate uncertainty. Failed calibration or no gain over matched random sampling falsifies the operational claim.
